Minor readability nit: `startSpread`/`endSpread` are written as fractions (0.25/0.05) and then multiplied by a bare `* 100` to become points. Directly above, `withNormalizedOffset` uses 0.5 as a genuine *normalized* offset, so a maintainer could read these 0.25/0.05 values the same way and edit them as normalized offsets — introducing a silent off-by-100. Consider expressing the two endpoints directly in points (e.g. `25`/`5`) and dropping the `* 100`, or renaming to make the unit explicit.
