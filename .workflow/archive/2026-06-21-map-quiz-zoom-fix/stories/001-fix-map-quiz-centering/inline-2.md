Non-blocking (units approximation): `maximumDistance` on `MapCameraBounds` is MapKit's camera-to-center distance (the camera's `distance` property), not the ground span shown on screen. `cameraDistance(for:)` computes the region's ground extent in metres and feeds it directly as the distance cap — these two quantities are only roughly proportional for a top-down imagery camera, not equal. This is fine for the fix's purpose: the real failure was order-of-magnitude (tens of degrees of overlay vs a ~12° region), and the 1.15 headroom plus the region's own generous padding (0.18 margin, 0.30 vertical inset, conservative aspect) absorb the approximation. Worth a one-line doc note that the cap is an approximation of camera distance, not an exact derivation, so a future reader doesn't treat the metres value as physically precise.
